# QA notes: Egyptian room audio (EG pack)

Only EG-100 (replacement) and the 4-line audition were listened to by Karim. All other clips were checked automatically only.

What was checked
- Coverage: 199 MP3 files, EG-001 to EG-200 except EG-016 (purr, left out on purpose). No file is missing and no extra file exists. Every manifest row maps to one file.
- Voice: each clip generated separately with the voice Sulafat and the same Egyptian teacher style as the approved audition.
- Loudness: clips were levelled to about -16.5 dB mean volume (range after levelling -16.9 to -15.9), peaks limited. Output is 44.1 kHz mono MP3.
- Pauses: no clip has a silent gap longer than 0.8 seconds in the middle.
- Length: 1.1 to 4.9 seconds.
- Spoken check by transcription on 8 clips (EG-009, 030, 047, 100, 114, 150, 187, 197): 7 matched in meaning, with small spelling or punctuation differences only. EG-009 and EG-030 came back as the meow word "miau/مياو", which is right. EG-100 (the original two-word "سجادة زرقا") came back as another language and Karim listened and found it unintelligible. EG-100 was replaced after his listening: new text "دي سجادة زرقا حلوة أوي", regenerated with the same voice, and Karim approved the new clip. It is the only clip a person has listened to.
- The other 191 clips were not transcribed. Transcription of Arabic is not proof that the pronunciation is right.
